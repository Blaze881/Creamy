Download Creamy Launcher

Creamy Launcher brings the look and feel of Android 4.0.4, Ice Cream Sandwich, to phones running Android 16.

Download Creamy Launcher

Requires Android 16.

Install

  1. Download the APK and open it. If asked, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  2. Tap Install, then Open.
  3. Press the Home button and choose Creamy Launcher as your home app, then tap Always.

Uninstall

Open Settings › Apps › Default apps › Home app, choose another launcher, then uninstall Creamy Launcher like any other app.
