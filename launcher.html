Android 4.0.4

Creamy Launcher brings the look and feel of Android 4.0.4, Ice Cream Sandwich, to Android 16. The lock screen, widgets, notifications, multi-tasking and everything in between has been rethought and refined to make your phone simple, beautiful, and beyond smart.

Face Unlock

Android 4.0 introduced Face Unlock: switch on your phone, look into the camera and unlock it with a smile. No complicated passwords to remember.

Android Beam

Android Beam lets you share contacts, websites, apps, maps, directions and YouTube videos with people close by. Simply hold two NFC enabled Android phones close to each other and touch to beam and share.

Voice typing

Watch text instantly appear as you speak with voice input. Touch the microphone on the keyboard and use your voice to instantly type your emails, SMS, or anywhere you want to enter text.

Google+

With Google+ built in, sharing the right things with the right people is a lot easier. With Messenger, you can bring groups of friends together into a simple group conversation and with Hangouts you can video chat with up to 9 people wherever you are.

Single-Motion
Panoramic Camera

The Android 4.0 camera brings zero shutter-lag, automatic focus, top notch low-light performance and a simple, intuitive way to take panoramic pictures. No stitching needed. Take amazing photos or 1080p videos, edit and then share them right from your phone.

Vibrant high-definition display

Android 4.0 was designed for high-definition screens, making text, videos & games look crisp, vibrant and rich. Creamy Launcher scales the classic Ice Cream Sandwich interface to the sharp, edge-to-edge displays of modern phones.

Slim and curved design

Ice Cream Sandwich’s clean, no-nonsense design puts content first, with a minimal System Bar and virtual buttons that stay out of the way. Creamy Launcher keeps that simple, uncluttered look on Android 16.

Fast & smooth

Quickly switch between apps with the Recent Apps list and enjoy fluid animations throughout the interface. Creamy Launcher is built to keep the classic Android 4.0.4 experience light and responsive.
